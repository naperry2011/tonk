Tonk Privacy Policy

Effective October 11, 2026

Tonk is a card game you play on your own device against computer opponents. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Tonk app for Android and iOS.

The short version

Tonk does not collect, send, sell, or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no internet access. Everything it remembers stays on your device.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your game progress: the match in progress, the daily challenge in progress, your level, experience points, chip bankroll, statistics, and achievements.
  • Your settings: table and card styles, sound and haptics preferences, and the nickname the computer opponents call you.

This information is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. It is never transmitted to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Chips are play money

Chips in Tonk have no real-world value. They cannot be bought, sold, cashed out, or exchanged for money or prizes. Tonk does not offer real-money gambling.

Children

Tonk is intended for adults and is not directed at children. Because the app collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Tonk changes how information is handled (for example, by adding online play), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
